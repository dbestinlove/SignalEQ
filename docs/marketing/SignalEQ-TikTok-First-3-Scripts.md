# SignalEQ TikTok — First 3 Video Scripts

Read these as a base, not a script to memorize word-for-word — say it in your own voice once you know the beats. Timing estimates assume natural speaking pace (~2.5 words/sec).

---

## Video 1: "Why I'm building this" (The Idea)
**Pillar:** Idea | **Target length:** ~25-30 sec | **Visual:** You could show a few "best phone 2026" articles/thumbnails, then cut to your equalizer UI

**Script:**

> "Every 'best phone' article is lying to you.
>
> Not on purpose — they just picked someone else's priorities and called it objective. Best camera? Best battery? Best value? Those all point to different phones, and the article picked one and called it 'best.'
>
> So I'm building something that doesn't pick for you. You move the sliders — battery, camera, price, whatever you actually care about — and it ranks phones by YOUR definition of best. Not mine, not some editor's.
>
> It's called SignalEQ. I'm building it solo, and I'm posting the whole thing here. Follow if you want to see it happen."

**On-screen text for hook (first 2 sec):** "Every 'best phone' article is lying to you"

## Video 3: The battery slider "lie" (Process)
**Pillar:** Process | **Target length:** ~30-35 sec | **Visual:** Could be voiceover over you looking at spec sheets, or simple screen text with numbers

**Script:**

> "Battery capacity numbers on spec sheets are basically useless. Here's why.
>
> A 5000 mAh battery on one phone can outlast a 5000 mAh battery on another by hours — because of the chip efficiency, the screen, what's running in the background. Same number, totally different real-world battery life.
>
> So when I built the battery weight into SignalEQ, I couldn't just use the raw mAh number. I had to think about what actually predicts real battery life, and weight it that way.
>
> This is the kind of decision that goes into every slider. It's not just 'bigger number = better.' If it were that simple, everyone would've built this already."

**On-screen text for hook:** "That battery number on the spec sheet? Basically useless."

## Notes for filming
- Record hook line 2-3 times, pick the take with the most energy — it matters more than the rest of the video combined.
- Don't over-edit early videos. Rough-but-real fits the build-in-public pillar better than polished.
- Save these three as a "part 1/2/3" mental thread even if you don't label them that way on-screen — it gives you a natural reason to reference back in later videos.
