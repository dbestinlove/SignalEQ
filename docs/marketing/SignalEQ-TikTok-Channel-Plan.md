# SignalEQ TikTok Channel — Launch Plan

**Paste this at the start of content-planning sessions. Update Status at the bottom weekly.**

## Purpose
A dedicated, US-registered channel that builds an audience around SignalEQ's development — in public, in real time. The channel's job: prove there are people who care about "best is a function of your values, not a spec sheet," before and while SignalEQ launches. Two outcomes are both wins — future users, and credibility/visibility that opens other doors. Let the channel tell you which one it's leaning toward; don't force it early.

## Audience
People into phones/tech who are tired of "best phone" listicles that don't match their priorities, plus people who like watching things get built (indie dev / build-in-public audience). These are two different TikTok crowds that can both work — content pillars below cover both.

## Content Pillars
Rotate across these so the channel doesn't feel one-note:

1. **Build-in-Public** — screen recordings of actual SignalEQ progress: sliders working, a bug you fixed, a decision you made and why. Authentic, unpolished is fine — it's the point.
2. **The Idea** — short, punchy explainers of the core concept ("why 'best phone' is a lie," "your priorities ≠ mine, so why is the ranking the same"). This is your hook content — most shareable, least effort once you have a few.
3. **Process/Voiceover Teardown** — you narrating a decision: why this weight, why this data source, why this UI choice. This is where your "web-thinking" style is actually an asset — viewers like seeing reasoning, not just results.
4. **Personal/Behind the Build** (occasional) — you, the person doing this, no product talk. Builds trust faster than product content alone. Save for once camera-comfort increases.

Ratio to start: roughly 40% Build-in-Public, 30% The Idea, 20% Process, 10% Personal.

## Format & Style
- Start: voiceover over screen recordings / footage. No pressure to be on camera yet.
- First 2-3 seconds must state the hook out loud, not just show it — TikTok viewers decide to stay or scroll almost instantly.
- Keep most videos 15-45 seconds early on. Short and finished beats long and half-baked.
- Captions on always (many watch muted).
- One idea per video. Resist cramming.

## Posting Cadence (the ramp)
- **Weeks 1-2:** 3 posts/week. Goal: build the habit without burning out, get a feel for what performs.
- **Weeks 3-4:** 4-5 posts/week if it's still feeling good, not forced.
- **Month 2+:** daily/near-daily *if* sustainable — but only increase cadence after you've held the previous one steady for 2 weeks. Never increase and change content style in the same week.
- If you skip a day, don't "make up for it" by doubling up — just post the next scheduled one. Guilt-posting produces worse content.

## First 2 Weeks — Content Ideas (pick and adapt, don't script word-for-word)
1. "Why I'm building a phone comparison app that refuses to tell you what's best" (Idea)
2. Screen recording: dragging a slider, showing rankings shift live (Build-in-Public)
3. "The battery slider is lying to you and here's why" — walk through one weight's effect (Process)
4. "Here's the actual data problem nobody talks about" — CSV/data cleaning story (Build-in-Public)
5. Quick take: a recent phone release, run through your own framework, not a review (Idea)
6. "I broke the live update yesterday, here's what happened" (Build-in-Public, vulnerability content — performs well)
7. Origin story, short version: why you started SignalEQ (Personal)

## Growth Mechanics
- Every video needs one clear thing to do at the end: follow for progress, comment your priority (battery vs camera vs price), or "wait for part 2."
- Turn your best-performing video into a short series (part 1/2/3) — series drive follows more than one-offs.
- Reply to comments on-camera/voiceover as their own short videos when a comment sparks something — cheap, high-engagement content.
- Don't chase trends/sounds that don't fit the pillars above. A slightly smaller, correct audience beats a bigger, wrong one — same logic as SignalEQ itself.

## Metrics to Watch (don't over-index on any single one early)
- **Retention** (watch time / completion rate) — the single best signal of whether content is actually working, more than view count.
- Follower growth week over week
- Which pillar is outperforming — let data tell you where to lean, not assumption
- Comments/saves (saves especially signal "useful," which matters for this niche)

## Risks
- **Burnout from over-ramping** → mitigated by the cadence plan above; resist the urge to start at daily.
- **Content feels like ads for SignalEQ, turns people off** → mitigated by pillar ratio (60% isn't direct product pitch).
- **No signal yet on what resonates** → mitigated by posting variety early (weeks 1-2), then narrowing based on retention data.
